# GoTo Devcamp 2023 Hackathon - GoHealing (Group 4)

## Backgrounder

Traveling and/or entertainment are the two things that are popular with the public. According to the data from the Ministry of Tourism and Creative Economy of the Republic of Indonesia, in the first semester of 2023, there was around 12.57% increase in Indonesian tourist visits compared to 2022 [[1]](https://kemenparekraf.go.id/en/articles/expert-survey-tourism-and-creative-economy-sectors-to-grow-in-2024). Furthermore, according to data published by the World Economy Forum in 2022, Indonesia ranked 32nd out of 117 countries on the Travel and Tourism Competitiveness Index in 2021. There is an increase of up to 12 ranks compared to last year [[2]](https://www.weforum.org/publications/travel-and-tourism-development-index-2021/in-full/executive-summary-f23acad558/). This shows that there is a significant improvement of the Indonesian tourism sector.

But, on the other side, some problems are currently faced by people related to traveling and/or entertainment. According to our mini survey result, we identified that there are problems related to the inefficiency of the itinerary booking process. For instance, when a person buys a movie ticket via an online ticketing platform, he/she usually still needs to order the ride-hailing service from another platform to be able to get to the cinema location. This inefficiency often makes people feel overwhelmed, especially when the price is quite expensive. The other problem is still related to the previous one, where there could be some waiting time experienced by people, like for example when changing from one transportation to another one. These problems could result in a decrease in national income from this promising sector if there is no breakthrough solution. 

These problems need to be tackled with such an application feature that could enable people to purchase their desired itinerary along with its supporting transportation at once at a reasonable price so that it could introduce a new level of seamless tourism mobility in Indonesia and maximize the national income from this sector.
